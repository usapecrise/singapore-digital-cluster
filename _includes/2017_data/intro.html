Background

As technology application expands across all sectors of society, transportation systems are no exception. Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS), which use data and communication tools to help improve the efficiency, safety, and resilience of transportation systems, are quickly proliferating across APEC economies. While these systems offer many benefits, they also introduce complex cybersecurity risks that can disrupt trade, endanger public safety, and erode trust in digital operations. Equipping government and industry actors to govern, prepare for, prevent, detect, respond to, and recover from cybersecurity incidents across aviation, maritime, rail, transit, and road sectors is critical. As evidenced by the 2025 APEC Digital and AI Ministerial Statement, APEC recognizes the urgent need to address cybersecurity risks and continues to advance safe, secure, and reliable digital and AI ecosystems.

Purpose

Through the APEC Transportation Working Group, the United States is leading a project on building cyber-resilient ITS, which aims to improve operational readiness, human awareness, and cyber hygiene across APEC. Through two regional dialogues and the development of a Transport Cyber Hygiene Toolkit, this project will support APEC economies to lay the groundwork for starting to develop a regional Transport Cybersecurity Roadmap. This regional dialogue is the first initiative under this line of effort. The event will convene public and private sector representatives to exchange views and practical experiences on transportation cybersecurity across APEC. Guided by established frameworks, it will explore various policy, operational, and human capital levers for advancing transportation cybersecurity, including how AI can be leveraged for cyber defense. The dialogue will also feature discussions on a coordinated APEC response to cross-border cybersecurity threats.

Participants

The dialogue is intended for transportation and cybersecurity policymakers as well as technical experts and industry representatives in transportation, cybersecurity, critical infrastructure, and operational resilience across APEC economies.
