APEC Workshop on Digital Twin Standards and Applications for Manufacturing

September 28–29, 2026 • 1.5 Days
View Details

This workshop will bring together policymakers, standards officials, and private-sector representatives from across the APEC region to strengthen understanding of digital twin standards and the application of digital twins in the manufacturing sector.

Through expert presentations, case studies, live demonstrations, and interactive discussions, participants will explore how digital twin standards can improve product design, predictive maintenance, and operational efficiency. The workshop will also provide a forum for economies to exchange experiences, discuss challenges and opportunities, and identify approaches to support effective adoption and alignment of digital twin standards.

Trust

Mechanisms for Data Protection & Cross-Border Data Flows in Cloud Transformation

September 30, 2026 • 1 Day
View Details

Cloud technologies are increasingly enabling digital transformation by supporting the adoption of emerging technologies, enhancing operational efficiency, and facilitating innovation across sectors.

As organizations rely on cloud-based platforms to store, process, and share data across jurisdictions, economies are seeking effective mechanisms to protect data, address cybersecurity risks, and enable trusted cross-border data flows. This workshop will bring together policymakers and industry representatives to exchange experiences and explore policy, regulatory, and technical approaches that support secure cloud adoption, trusted data governance, and regional digital connectivity.

Cloud-Ready Workforce Training & Public-Private Partnerships for Workforce Skilling

October 1, 2026 • 1 Day
View Details

As economies accelerate the adoption of digital technologies, developing a workforce with cloud-related technical, operational, and cybersecurity competencies has become an increasingly important priority.

Addressing skills gaps and ensuring access to relevant training opportunities requires collaboration among governments, industry, academia, and workforce development organizations. This workshop will bring together policymakers, workforce development leaders, and industry stakeholders to explore how public-private partnerships can strengthen cloud-ready workforce capabilities and expand access to digital skills training.
