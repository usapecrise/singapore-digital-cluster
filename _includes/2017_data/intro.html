FOCUS
Digital Transformation Across APEC
Background

As economies increasingly rely on digital technologies, emerging tools such as artificial intelligence, cloud technologies, and advanced digital infrastructure are transforming how governments, businesses, and workers operate. These technologies offer significant opportunities for innovation and economic growth, while also raising important considerations around trust, security, governance, and workforce readiness.

Purpose

The Technology, Trust, and Talent: Advancing Digital Transformation Across APEC Workshop Series brings together policymakers, technical experts, and private-sector representatives to exchange practical approaches for advancing secure, trusted, and inclusive digital transformation across APEC economies.

Through expert presentations, case studies, and interactive discussions, the workshop series examines emerging technologies and their applications, approaches to strengthening trust and security in digital environments, and the skills and workforce capabilities needed to support digital transformation.

Participants

The workshop series is intended for policymakers, regulators, technical experts, industry representatives, and other stakeholders involved in digital policy, emerging technologies, cybersecurity, workforce development, and digital transformation across APEC economies.
