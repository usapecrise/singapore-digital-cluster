Workshops

Explore the three workshops focused on technology, trust, and talent. Participants may attend individual workshops or the full series.

Technology

APEC Workshop on Digital Twin Standards and Applications for Manufacturing

September 28–29, 2026 • 1.5 Days

This workshop will bring together policymakers, standards officials, and private-sector representatives from across APEC to strengthen understanding of digital twin standards and their application in manufacturing. Through expert presentations, case studies, live demonstrations, and interactive discussions, participants will explore how digital twins can improve product design, predictive maintenance, and operational efficiency.

The workshop will also provide a forum for economies to exchange experiences, discuss implementation challenges and opportunities, and identify approaches to support effective adoption and alignment of digital twin standards.

Digital Twins Standards Manufacturing
Trust

Mechanisms for Data Protection & Cross-Border Data Flows in Cloud Transformation

September 30, 2026 • 1 Day

As organizations increasingly rely on cloud platforms to store, process, and share data across jurisdictions, economies are seeking effective mechanisms to protect data, address cybersecurity risks, and enable trusted cross-border data flows.

This workshop will bring together policymakers and industry representatives to exchange experiences and explore policy, regulatory, and technical approaches that support secure cloud adoption, trusted data governance, and regional digital connectivity.

Data Protection Cloud Security Data Flows
Talent

Cloud-Ready Workforce Training & Public-Private Partnerships for Workforce Skilling

October 1, 2026 • 1 Day

As economies accelerate digital technology adoption, developing a workforce with cloud-related technical, operational, and cybersecurity competencies is becoming increasingly important.

This workshop will bring together policymakers, workforce development leaders, and industry stakeholders to explore how public-private partnerships can strengthen cloud-ready workforce capabilities and expand access to digital skills training.
